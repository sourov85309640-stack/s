SWAP MAP  -  Roofing master v3.1 (light, interactive homepage). Sample firm: Ridgewell Roofing, Cirencester.
Every sample item carries data-sample="..." in the markup: search the built file (or src/) for  data-sample
Items the owner must confirm with the client carry data-confirm="...".
Source: src/ . Build the single file with:  python3 build.py roofing-master-v3.1.html
Each section lives in its own file: src/sections/NAME.html  +  src/NAME.css  +  src/NAME.js

BEFORE SENDING TO A CLIENT
  1. Delete the owner tool "Design options": in src/index.html remove the two lines marked OWNER TOOL
     (options.css and options.js). Fix your choices first (see DESIGN OPTIONS below).
  2. Replace every data-sample item below. Delete any sample module the client cannot back with real content.
  3. Set the form endpoint (form#enquiry action="REPLACE-ENDPOINT").

PHOTOS (honest note)
  All photos in src/img are stock or placeholder images of unknown licence carried over from earlier rounds (Unsplash could not be reached
  from this build environment). They are for illustration only, never the client's work. The footer says so. Confirm the licence or replace
  them with the client's own photos before a site goes live.

DESIGN OPTIONS (owner tool, bottom-left "Design options" button)
  Each section keeps a default and one or two alternatives. The panel adds ?param=N to the address. To fix a choice for a client, set
  data-v="N" on that <section> in src/sections/NAME.html (hero layout: data-hero="a|b|c" on <html> in src/index.html), then delete the panel.
  Hero layout   ?hero=   a headline left (default) | b gable plate with text card | c centred with rating chip
  Hero motion   ?hm=     1 calm roofscape (default) | 2 roof assembles | 6 wind
  Reviews       ?rv=     1 card rail (default) | 2 card deck | 6 cover flow
  Services      ?sv=     1 lead card and list (default) | 2 roof slope of tiles | 6 chooser
  Where water   ?wv=     2 follow the water (default) | 3 rain and loupe | 6 camera zoom
  Decide        ?dv=     2 weathering roof (default) | 1 gauge | 4 dial
  3D roof       ?xv=     1 layers lift apart (default) | 2 roof builds itself | 5 raindrop through the layers
  Project       ?pv=     1 facts list (default) | 2 before and after drawing
  How it works  ?hv=     1 timeline (default) | 2 ridge walk | 4 quote fills in
  Checks        ?cv=     1 sightline diagram (default) | 4 binocular photo
  Areas         ?av=     1 contour map (default) | 2 rooftops
  FAQ           ?fv=     1 accordion (default) | 2 answer panel
  Contact       ?ctv=    4 evening house (default) | 2 clipboard | 5 roof outline draws
  Atmosphere    ?fxv=    6 full (default) | 3 weather story | 1 particles only | 0 off

3D ROOF COVERING
  The "Tiles | Slates" switch swaps the covering artwork and its name. Default: data-cover="tiles" on #whole. Layer names and copy are in
  src/sections/whole.html and are neutral (Ridge and lead, Covering, Battens, Underlay, Rafters).

MOTION
  Turn every animation off for a client who prefers a still page: <html data-motion="off">. Visitors with reduced motion get the still page
  automatically. The page reads fully without JavaScript.

OPTIONAL MODULES (not in the page)  docs/optional_modules.html: proof strip (real accreditations only), specialism band, team note, before and after.
