## Bugs
D1 MED mobile: fixed .bar covers focused FAQ buttons. 390x844, Tab to q6: button 788-844, bar top 788 (q5 797 half covered). WCAG 2.4.11. Cause: styles.css:52 html scroll-padding-top only. Fix: add scroll-padding-bottom:5rem under the max-width where .bar shows (about 4.5rem); also 2rem on desktop (D2).
D2 LOW desktop: focus ring clipped at viewport bottom. 1280x720, Tab to q2: bottom 720 = viewport edge. Same fix as D1.
D3 MED touch: sticky hover colour. styles.css:372 `.q button:hover{color:var(--copper)}` is not inside @media (hover:hover). 390 touch: after tapping a question closed, hover:hover=false but colour stays copper rgb(152,86,50). Fix: wrap in @media(hover:hover).
D4 LOW/MED find-in-page cannot find answer text: window.find('rotten battens') false while closed (visibility:hidden); questions are found. Cause: styles.css:373-375 hidden state. Fix options: hidden="until-found" plus beforematch listener that opens the .q (needs height animation by JS since content-visibility replaces grid trick), or accept and say so.
D5 LOW deep links: #q4 opens nothing, #a4 scrolls to a 0px hidden target (y 11232, lands in the middle of the list). Fix in app.js (~47-54): on load and hashchange, if hash matches #q1..#a6 open that .q; add scroll-margin-top to .q.
D6 LOW areas list: dangling "/" at line ends at every width where it wraps (1280: "Swindon /" then "Lechlade"; 390: "Fairford /", "Stroud /"; 768 too). Cause: styles.css:528 li::after separator. Fix: drop separators and use gap, or draw the slash as ::before and clip rows' first slash with overflow clip/negative margin trick.
D7 LOW in-page links land 80px low: nav click to #faq/#how/#projects leaves section top at 144px (intended 64); direct #hash load gives 80. Cause: Lenis 1.3.26 subtracts html scroll-padding-top (5rem) on top of motion.js offset -64. Verified: with scroll-padding-top 0 lands at 64. Fix: offset 0 in scrollTo for Lenis (let scroll-padding supply 80), or remove the extra -HDR.
D8 LOW rules: .a transition .45s and chevron .4s exceed the brief's UI <300ms; accordion animates grid-template-rows (layout), not transform/opacity (accepted in brief, only noting).
D9 LOW a11y: without JS aria-expanded="false" is in markup while panels show open (mismatch); 6 role="region" panels create landmark noise (WCAG fine, best-practice says avoid for 6+). Fix: set aria-expanded from app.js, drop role=region.
D10 LOW content/visual: chimney.webp is used twice (stage 1 and #checks image); stage images 112px are very small on desktop (square crops of 328x180 style sources, fine but not a hero); #areas has about 100px empty below the note at 1280 (looks sparse); areas li hover turns copper though not links; how-intro says "written summary at the end of the first two" which is an unverified claim about Inspect (flag for owner).
