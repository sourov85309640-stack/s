Looking after your roof.

A few minutes each season, mostly from the ground, saves most of the repairs we see. Here is what to look for and when.

Autumn

  • Clear leaves out of gutters, valleys and downpipe tops.
  • Look for slipped or cracked tiles before the winter storms.
  • Check the lead and pointing round the chimney.
  • Cut back branches that touch or overhang the roof.

Winter

  • After a storm, look for tiles or slates on the ground.
  • Watch ceilings and the tops of walls for new damp patches.
  • Drips in the loft on frosty mornings are often condensation, not a leak.
  • Never go up on a wet or icy roof. Call us instead.

Spring

  • Look for moss and lichen building up on the tiles.
  • Check gutters once nesting birds have moved on.
  • Look along the ridge and verges for loose or missing mortar.
  • Plan bigger jobs for the drier months.

Summer

  • Settled weather is the best time for re-roofing and bigger repairs.
  • Check flat roofs for blisters and cracks after hot spells.
  • Clean or repaint fascias and soffits.
  • Have a survey done while it is easy to see everything.
