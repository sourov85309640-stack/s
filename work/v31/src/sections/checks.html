Three things you can check from the ground.

Stand in the garden or the street. Please do not climb up.

  1. Slates and tiles

    Look along the roof for gaps, slipped or cracked pieces and green patches of moss.

    Slates and tiles looked at

  2. Chimney and lead

    Check the pointing, the pots and the grey lead strip where the stack meets the roof.

    Chimney and lead looked at

  3. Gutters and walls

    Look for stains below the gutter and plants growing from the joints.
