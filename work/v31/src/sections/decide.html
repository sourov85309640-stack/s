Repair, maintain, investigate or replace.

The right recommendation should follow the condition of the roof, not the size of the job.

  1. Repair

    A few slipped slates or a split flashing on an otherwise sound roof. Fix the fault and leave the rest alone.

  2. Maintain

    Moss in the valleys, blocked gutters, tired pointing. Clear and re-point before they turn into leaks.

  3. Investigate

    Stains with no clear source, or a roof you cannot see. Survey the loft and the roof before anyone quotes.

  4. Replace

    Battens rotting, slates delaminating, nails sliding. Past that point patching costs more than a new roof.
