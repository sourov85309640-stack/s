Roofs sometimes hide rotten battens or a failed valley once the tiles are off. The usual approach is to stop, show you photos, explain the options and agree the cost in writing before any extra work starts.

A few slates or a section of ridge is usually a few hours to a day. A full re-roof on an average house is more often one to two weeks, depending on size and weather.

Beyond a small repair at low level, scaffolding is normally needed for safe access. It is often the largest single line on a roofing quote, so check whether it is listed separately.

Roofing stops in heavy rain, high wind and frost, because wet slates are unsafe underfoot and some materials will not bond. Expect dates to move with the forecast.

Ask for any guarantee in writing, with what it covers and for how long. New tiles and slates usually carry a manufacturer's guarantee. Cover for the workmanship varies from firm to firm.

Send photos and a few details through the form, or call. Most roofs need a visit before a firm price, because a photo cannot show the loft or the battens.
