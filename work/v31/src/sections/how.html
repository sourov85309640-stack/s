From first look to handover.

Four steps, always in this order, so you know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Inspect

    A look at the roof from a ladder or scaffold, then into the loft. Slates, ridge, valleys, flashings and gutters are checked and photographed.

  2. 02

    Quote

    A written quote that names the fault, the fix and the materials, and lists what is left out so nothing arrives as a surprise.

  3. 03

    Work

    Old tiles come off, new battens, underlay and tiles go on. The roof is planned so it is not left open overnight.

    If something new is uncovered, it is explained before any extra work is added.

  4. 04

    Handover

    Gutters and drive are cleared and you walk round the roof from the ground. Photos of the finished work follow.
