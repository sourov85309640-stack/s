6 working days

A recent project in Fairford

Roof
Cotswold stone-slate cottage, three gables
Problem
A failed lead valley by the chimney and slipped stone slates let water into the first-floor bedroom.
Work
Stripped the affected slope, relaid the valley in new lead and re-hung the slates, adding matching stone where needed.
Materials
Reclaimed stone slates, code 5 lead, treated battens, breathable underlay
Duration
Six working days

Scaffold up and the slope sheeted over.
