What one slipped slate does.

  1. It starts with one slate. A quiet evening. The roof has kept the rain out for years, but one nail has quietly rusted through.
  2. A storm lifts it. Wind gets under the loose slate and it slides out. From the ground you might never notice.
  3. Water finds the gap. Rain runs in, along the felt and the timbers, and shows up as a stain somewhere else.
  4. Left for a winter. Wet battens rot, more slates slip, and a small repair becomes a bigger one.
  5. Caught early, it is a morning's work. The slate goes back, the rest of the roof is checked, and the ceiling dries out. Tell us what you have spotted
