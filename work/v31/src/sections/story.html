What one slipped slate does.

  1. It starts with one slate. A calm evening. The roof has kept the rain out for years, but one nail has quietly rusted through.
  2. A storm lifts it. Wind gets under the loose slate and it slides out. From the ground you might never notice.
  3. Water finds the gap. Rain runs in, along the felt and the timbers, and shows up as a stain somewhere else.
  4. Get in touch as soon as you see it. Put a bucket under the drip and call or send the form straight away. While it is one slate and a damp patch, it is a small job.
  5. We look first, then talk it through. We check the ceiling and the roof, take photos, and show you what we found, the fix and the price before any work starts.
