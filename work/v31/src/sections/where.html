Where a roof lets water in.

Six places roofs fail. Most leaks start at one of them.

1 2 3 4 5 6 Survey
  1. Ridge Cracked mortar lets wind-driven rain in under the ridge tiles.
  2. Chimney stack Crumbling pointing and cracked flaunching soak the brickwork.
  3. Lead flashing Lifted or split lead lets water run straight in.
  4. Valley Worn lead or moss in a valley overflows into the timbers.
  5. Eaves Slipped tiles and torn felt soak the fascia and wall plate.
  6. Gutter A sagging or blocked gutter sends water down the wall.
