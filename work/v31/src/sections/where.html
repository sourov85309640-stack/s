Where a roof lets water in.

Six places roofs fail. Most leaks start at one of them.

1 2 3 4 5 6 Ridge
  1. 1Ridge. Cracked mortar or loose ridge tiles let wind-driven rain in at the top.
  2. 2Chimney stack. Crumbling pointing and a cracked top let rain soak down through the brickwork.
  3. 3Lead flashing. Lifted or split flashing where the roof meets a chimney or wall lets water run straight in.
  4. 4Valley. Where two slopes meet, a worn lining or a blocked channel spills water into the timbers.
  5. 5Eaves. Slipped tiles or slates and worn underlay at the bottom edge soak the fascia and the top of the wall.
  6. 6Gutter. A blocked or sagging gutter overflows and sends water down the wall.
